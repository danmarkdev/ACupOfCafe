Why we linger

Good coffee takes time. So do good conversations.

A Cup of Cafe started as a folding table and a hand grinder in a garage. We kept it small on purpose — every order is brewed fresh, not queued up in a batch, which means you might wait a minute longer for it. We think that's the point. Pull up a chair and stay a while.

"We're not trying to be fast. We're trying to be worth slowing down for."
